How commissions work

When you share a product with a markup, you earn the markup amount as commission when a buyer purchases through your link.

  1. Browse products and set your desired markup price
  2. Click "Share on WhatsApp" to generate your sales message
  3. Buyers contact the store with the marked-up price
  4. You collect the markup as your commission
